+ <card=Land Equilibrium> If an opponent who controls at least as many lands as you do would put a land onto the battlefield, that player instead puts that land onto the battlefield then sacrifices a land of their choice. => NounPhrase "at least as many lands as you do" as Object of control; PrepositionPhrase "as you do" as Complement of land; Clause "you do" as Complement of as

# A complete clause is no comparative clause
