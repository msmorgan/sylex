# Comparative, inequality, equative, and mixed-grade Adjective Phrases (and numbers bounded by comparatives, *10 or greater*, via CGEL, Ch. 15 §3.1, pp. 1323–1324, rule [1]) are never depictive adjuncts
+ <card=Mayael's Aria> Then you gain 10 life if you control a creature with power 10 or greater. => Amount "10 or greater" as Complement of power; !Depictive "10 or greater"; !PredicateAdjunct "10 or greater"; !AdjectivePhrase "10 or greater" as Adjunct of control; !AdjectivePhrase "10 or greater" as Adjunct of gain
+ <card=Sphere of Annihilation> At the beginning of your upkeep, exile this artifact, all creatures and planeswalkers with mana value less than or equal to the number of void counters on it, and all creature and planeswalker cards in graveyards with mana value less than or equal to the number of void counters on it. => !AdjectivePhrase "less than or equal to the number of void counters on it" as Adjunct of exile
+ <card=Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice> <legendary> Whenever an Aura you control enters, if you cast it, you may search your library for an Aura card with mana value less than or equal to that Aura and with a different name than each Aura you control, put that card onto the battlefield attached to Light-Paws, then shuffle. => !AdjectivePhrase "less than or equal to that Aura" as Adjunct of search; !AdjectivePhrase "less than or equal to that Aura" as Modifier of library
+ <card=Pteramander> {7}{U}: Adapt 4. This ability costs {1} less to activate for each instant and sorcery card in your graveyard. => NounPhrase "{1} less" as Object of cost; !AdjectivePhrase "less" as Adjunct of cost
