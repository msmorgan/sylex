# A Vote Option is a word a vote offers, cited [CR#701.38a,701.38b]. The two after *for* under *vote* are an OptionChoice; one cited later is an OptionMention, the Subject of a clause that counts its votes or the premodifier of *vote* (*each numbers vote*). A face's Vote Options are read from its text: the vote reads whatever two words stand after *for*, and a mention discharges it. A text that holds only one of the two states the words the rest of its face introduces and uses (`<vote=WORD>`)
+ <card=Bite of the Black Rose> <vote=sickness> <vote=psychosis> Will of the council — Starting with you, each player votes for sickness or psychosis. If sickness gets more votes, creatures your opponents control get -2/-2 until end of turn. If psychosis gets more votes or the vote is tied, each opponent discards two cards. => OptionChoice "sickness or psychosis" as Complement of for; OptionMention "psychosis"#2 as Subject of get; !NounPhrase "psychosis"#2; readings 1
+ <card=Lieutenants of the Guard> <vote=strength> <vote=numbers> Put a +1/+1 counter on this creature for each strength vote and create a 1/1 white Soldier creature token for each numbers vote. => NounPhrase "each numbers vote"; OptionMention "numbers" as Modifier of vote; !Nominal "numbers"
+ <card=Trial of a Time Lord> <vote=innocent> <vote=guilty> If guilty gets more votes, the owner of each card exiled with this Saga puts that card on the bottom of their library. => OptionMention "guilty" as Subject of get
+ <card=Path of the Enigma> <vote=planeswalk> <vote=chaos> If planeswalk gets more votes, planeswalk. => OptionMention "planeswalk"#1 as Subject of get; readings 1
+ <card=Expropriate> <vote=time> <vote=money> For each money vote, choose a permanent owned by the voter and gain control of it. => OptionMention "money" as Modifier of vote; !Nominal "money"; readings 1
