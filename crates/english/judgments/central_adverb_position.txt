# After *be*, a central adverb precedes its locative Complement as it does a predicative one (CGEL, Ch. 3 §2.1.5, p. 102; Ch. 4 §5.2, p. 257)
+ <card=Commander Spock> If that card is still on top of your library, draw a card. => AdverbPhrase "still" as Adjunct of be; PrepositionPhrase "on top of your library" as Complement of be
+ <card=Auratouched Mage> If this creature is still on the battlefield, put that Aura card onto the battlefield attached to it. => AdverbPhrase "still" as Adjunct of be; PrepositionPhrase "on the battlefield" as Complement of be; !PrepositionPhrase "on the battlefield" as PredicativeComplement of be
