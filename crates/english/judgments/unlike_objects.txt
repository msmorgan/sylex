# Unlike Objects: a coordination of unlike kinds is an Object or a preposition's Complement only where its head selects each kind there alone (CGEL, Ch. 15 §3.1, p. 1323, rule [1]); a slash measure is a conjunct only where the head selects a measure.
+ <card=Timber Paladin> As long as this creature is enchanted by exactly two Auras, it has base power and toughness 5/5 and vigilance. => NounPhrase "base power and toughness 5/5 and vigilance" as Object of have; NounPhrase "base power and toughness 5/5"; KeywordPhrase "vigilance"
+ <card=Goddric, Cloaked Reveler> Celebration — As long as two or more nonland permanents entered the battlefield under your control this turn, Goddric is a Dragon with base power and toughness 4/4, flying, and "{R}: Dragons you control get +1/+0 until end of turn." => NounPhrase "base power and toughness 4/4, flying, and \"{R}: Dragons you control get +1/+0 until end of turn.\"" as Complement of with; KeywordPhrase "flying"
+ <card=Tolaria> {T}: Target creature loses banding and all "bands with other" abilities until end of turn. Activate only during any upkeep step. => NounPhrase "banding and all \"bands with other\" abilities" as Object of lose; KeywordPhrase "banding"
# *Create* selects only a Noun Phrase: a keyword is no conjunct of its Object.
+ <card=Warden> Create a 4/4 white and blue Sphinx creature token with flying and vigilance. => NounPhrase "a 4/4 white and blue Sphinx creature token with flying and vigilance" as Object of create; !NounPhrase "a 4/4 white and blue Sphinx creature token with flying"
# *With* takes a keyword and a quoted ability together only as a Nominal's Modifier, the one host both its frames admit.
+ <card=Mirrex> {3}, {T}: Create a 1/1 colorless Phyrexian Mite artifact creature token with toxic 1 and "This token can't block." => PrepositionPhrase "with toxic 1 and \"This token can't block.\"" as Modifier of token; !PrepositionPhrase "with toxic 1 and \"This token can't block.\"" as Adjunct of create
