# A temporal clause locates its host's situation at the time of its own (CGEL,
# Ch. 8 §6, p. 700), so the two share a time sphere: past (a preterite) or
# non-past (a present, an imperative). A modal's preterite, ambiguous between
# past time and modal remoteness (Ch. 3 §2.4, p. 107), a plain form and a
# participle fix no time, and agree with either (§7, pp. 159–162). A conditional clause is independent of its host in time (Ch. 8
# §14.1.2, p. 743), and a Nominal has no time.

# A past temporal clause under a past host.
+ <card=Soulgorger Orgg> When this creature leaves the battlefield, you gain life equal to the life you lost when it entered. => PrepositionPhrase "when it entered" as Adjunct of lose; !PrepositionPhrase "when it entered" as Adjunct of gain
