card: Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar
oracle: power greater than its base power deals combat damage
corrected: power greater than its base power deal combat damage
evidence: Oracle errata of subject-verb agreement. The subject "one or more creatures you control each with power greater than its base power" is plural and takes "deal". The printed card (LCI 232) carries the same "deals" as Oracle, so this is not a printed-card disagreement; the correction restores the agreement every other "one or more creatures ... deal combat damage" trigger keeps.
