# Oracle editorial corrections: the census reads `corrected` in place of
# `oracle` in the named face's Oracle text. A correction is admitted only for
# an error in the text: the Oracle text disagrees with its printed card because
# of a data error (a dropped character, a wrong symbol), the Oracle text is
# ungrammatical English, or it writes a capital its structure does not call
# for or omits one it does (Realization derives each capital from the
# structure, `docs/design/english.md` §7). A template difference is never
# grounds for one (`docs/design/english.md` §4). Records are separated by a
# blank line; each has the four fields below, one line each. `oracle` occurs
# exactly once in the face's text, and a correction that matches no face is
# stale and fails `strata --check`.

card: Agency Outfitter
oracle: your graveyard, hand and/or library
corrected: your graveyard, hand, and/or library
evidence: The printed card (MKM 0038, Scryfall image 8112f133-535e-4264-8357-9cbf97957710) reads "graveyard, hand, and/or library", with the serial comma (`docs/oracle-style-guide.md` §3, "Commas and the serial comma"). Template siblings with that text in Oracle: Boonweaver Giant, Dark Supplicant, Mishra, Artificer Prodigy and Say Its Name. MTGJSON's `originalText` for MKM omits the comma, but the image shows it, so `originalText` is not evidence here.

card: Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar
oracle: power greater than its base power deals combat damage
corrected: power greater than its base power deal combat damage
evidence: Ungrammatical Oracle text: the subject "one or more creatures you control each with power greater than its base power" is plural, so the verb is "deal". The printed card (LCI 232) has the same "deals", so the ground is grammar, not a printed-card disagreement. Every other "one or more creatures ... deal combat damage" trigger in Oracle uses "deal".

card: Azlask, the Swelling Scourge
oracle: Scions and Spawns you control
corrected: Scions and Spawn you control
evidence: Ungrammatical Oracle text: Spawn has the zero plural. Hand of Emrakul, from Rise of the Eldrazi, the set that introduced the type, reads "sacrifice four Eldrazi Spawn", kept through every Oracle rewording, and no other in-scope Oracle text writes "Spawns". The printed card (M3C) has the same "Spawns", so the ground is grammar, not a printed-card disagreement.

card: Change of Plans
oracle: Each of X target creatures you control connive.
corrected: Each of X target creatures you control connives.
evidence: Ungrammatical Oracle text: fused distributive "each" heads the Subject, so the verb is singular; "connive" agrees with the nearer plural "creatures" instead. CGEL gives "each" singular agreement: it selects "a singular head" (Ch. 5 §7.4, p. 378), its fused-head partitive takes a singular verb ("Each (of them) was cut in two", p. 379 [23i]), and as a quantifier in a Subject it "enforce[s] singularity on the whole clause" (§19.3, p. 515 n. 75). The fused determiner-heads CGEL lets take a plural verb are "any", "none", "either" and "neither" (§18.3(f), p. 507), not "each". Every other "each of" Subject in the supported corpus whose verb shows Number, 40 of them, has a singular verb. The printed card (NCC 24) has the same "connive", so the ground is grammar, not a printed-card disagreement.

card: Necratog
oracle: graveyard: this creature gets
corrected: graveyard: This creature gets
evidence: A lower-case "this" opens the effect after an activated ability's colon, where Oracle capitalizes the first word of the effect as it opens a sentence. Necratog's is the only in-scope Oracle line with a lower-case letter right after ": ", and 58 other faces have ": This creature gets +2/+2 until end of turn.". The printed Weatherlight card (WTH 76) has no such word ("Remove the top creature card in your graveyard from the game: +2/+2 until end of turn", MTGJSON `originalText`), so the lower case came with the Oracle rewording.

card: Ogre Marauder
oracle: gains "this creature can't be blocked"
corrected: gains "This creature can't be blocked"
evidence: A quoted ability opens with a capital, as the sentence it is does: every other quoted ability in scope opens with one, as in Surge Engine's and Vronos, Masked Inquisitor's gains "This creature can't be blocked.". The lower-case quotations elsewhere in scope are Keyword Phrases ("bands with other legendary creatures.") and cited terms ("legend rule"), not abilities. The printed card (BOK 75) has no quotation ("it can't be blocked this turn unless defending player sacrifices a creature", MTGJSON `originalText`), so the lower case came with the Oracle rewording.
