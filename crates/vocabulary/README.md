# sylex_vocabulary

The core English lexicon and the game-vocabulary Catalogs, loaded as one
Lexical Environment: a `sylex_lexicon::Lexicon` and the
`sylex_syntax::FrameCatalog` of its Subcategorization Frames.

```rust
let environment = sylex_vocabulary::LexicalEnvironment::load()?;
let grammar = sylex_english::bind(&environment.lexicon, &environment.frames)?;
```

The design is in [Lexicon](../../docs/decisions/lexicon.md) and
[English](../../docs/decisions/english.md) §2; the terms are the
[glossary's](../../docs/contexts/oracle-english/CONTEXT.md).

## Data files

`lexicon/english/` holds Core vocabulary and `catalogs/` holds Catalog
vocabulary, all hand-authored RON embedded at build time.
[`src/schema.rs`](src/schema.rs) documents each authored type.

`data/gen/catalogs/card-names.txt` (the `name:` Lexemes) is generated by
`cargo xtask catalogs generate` and read at load. Shortened Names are computed
at load: `short_name` gives a face's candidate from its name, supertypes and
types, and `AttestedShortenings` keeps the candidates its own Oracle text uses,
read from `data/scryfall/oracle-cards.jsonl`.

## Adding a word

- **An ordinary English word** goes in the `lexicon/english/` file of its Part
  of Speech. An entry states only what the default spelling rules do not:
  irregular forms are overrides. Every verb outside `closed_class.ron` needs
  at least one frame, written in active voice with prepositions named by
  lemma, and every preposition it names must be declared.
- **A game word** goes in its catalog, citing the rule that defines it
  (`rule: "[CR#701.8]"`). The verbs, nouns and determinatives a keyword
  ability's rule defines (*cycle*, *crew*, *equipped*) are declared under that
  keyword in `keyword_abilities.ron`, never in the core lexicon.
- **An adjective** goes in `adjectives.ron`, unless a keyword action
  defines it (*transformed*, in `keyword_actions.ron`) or it names a status
  (*tapped*, in `statuses.ron`).
- **Card names** are generated by `cargo xtask catalogs generate` and
  Shortened Names are computed from the corpus at load; see
  [Lexicon](../../docs/decisions/lexicon.md) §6. What is hand-curated is
  the card-scoped words
  (`introduced_names.ron`, `cited_names.ron`, `vote_options.ron`,
  `anchor_words.ron`).

The loader refuses a Core word that is also a Catalog item of the same
Category, and `cr_coverage` refuses a catalog item the CR does not enumerate.

## Inspecting

```sh
cargo xtask lexical --card-name 'Jace, the Mind Sculptor' \
  --output /tmp/lexical.json --export /tmp/lexemes.ron
cargo run -p sylex_lexicon --example inspect -- /tmp/lexemes.ron 'cycle'
```

The first command analyzes the selected faces (`--all` for the corpus) and
exports every declared Lexeme; the second lists the Readings of a spelling.
`cargo xtask english parse 'TEXT'` shows how the grammar reads a line.

## Tests

- `cr_coverage`: each catalog against the CR's enumeration
  (`data/rules/cr.txt`; ability words also against
  `data/catalogs/ability-words.json`), with the out-of-scope items named in
  the test.
- `paradigms`: every verb and noun has its full paradigm, and every value
  realizes and analyzes back.
- `environment`: the whole environment loads and analyzes as declared.
- `labels`, `names`, `loader`: registered-label exclusivity, name scope, and
  the loader's rejections.

The lint that keeps catalog words out of the grammar is `sylex_english`'s
[`tests/catalog_leak.rs`](../english/tests/catalog_leak.rs).
