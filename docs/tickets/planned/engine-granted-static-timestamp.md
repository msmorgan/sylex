# A granted static ability's effects take the granting effect's timestamp when it is later

## Why

A continuous effect generated by a static ability has the timestamp of the object the ability is
on, or of the effect that created the ability, whichever is later [CR#613.7a]. `gather` in
`crates/engine/src/layer.rs` stamps every static's effect with its object's timestamp, including
a static the object acquired from a layer-6 grant. So the CR's own example comes out wrong: Rune of
Flight, attached later, grants Colossus Hammer "Equipped creature has flying"; that ability shares
Rune of Flight's timestamp, so its flying applies after the Hammer's "loses flying" and the
equipped creature flies. The engine orders the granted flying by the Hammer's earlier timestamp,
and the creature loses it. The same gap reaches Myth Realized: its activation grants itself a
power-and-toughness ability that should override set effects which began after Myth Realized
entered but before the activation (Myth Realized's 2015-02-25 ruling).

## Done when

- An acquired static's effects carry the later of its object's timestamp and the granting
  effect's timestamp, and when the object gets a new timestamp, those effects keep their relative
  order [CR#613.7a].
- `sylex_engine` tests: Rune of Flight on Colossus Hammer gives the equipped creature flying
  [CR#613.7a]; Myth Realized activated after a later-entered Godhead of Awe has power and
  toughness equal to its lore counters.

## Pointers

- `crates/engine/src/layer.rs`: `gather` (the `timestamp` it gives each static's effect),
  `EffectOrigin`, `ActiveEffect::acquired`, the `GainAbility` arm of `apply_static`.
- `crates/engine/tests/layers.rs`: `myth_realized_gained_definition_is_not_characteristic_defining`.
